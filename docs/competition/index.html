Competition rules

Competition rules

Entry

Using the Qualcomm Dragonwing™ Virtual Platform in QBox, demonstrate a use case with Qualcomm® Linux® and Qualcomm Neural Network (QNN) models from the Qualcomm® AI Hub. Explain what it does, how to run it, and show it working. Entries are judged on a submitted 30-second video. Send the video to competition@dvcon-europe.org.

Individual and team entries are allowed. Entrants must get their collaborators' consent before naming them or submitting their work.

Schedule

  • Closing date: 26 February 2027.
  • Results: Results will be announced at a SystemC Evolution Fika in March 2027.

Prizes

Prizes for the best entries: Winners will receive Arduino VENTUNO Q.

Judging

Judges will consider relevance to the Qualcomm Dragonwing™ Virtual Platform in QBox and the Qualcomm® Linux® and QNN use case; functionality shown; clarity of the explanation; and creativity.

Originality and third-party rights

Entries must be entrants' own original work. Use of models from the Qualcomm® AI Hub is encouraged.

DVCon copyright form

An entry must be accompanied by a completed DVCon copyright form.
